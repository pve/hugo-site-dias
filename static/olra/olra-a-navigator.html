OLRA-A NavigatorDraft

Agentic edition of OLRA (OWASP LLM Risk Allocation) · runs side by side with the OLRA (original) navigator

Who does what when an AI agent goes wrong? OLRA-A takes the ten threat categories of the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications (ASI01–ASI10), keeps the highest-value mitigations for each, and allocates every control to one of six AI supply-chain roles. Use it to explore where ownership concentrates, or switch on Tabletop mode to hide the owners and discuss them first.

How to read this

What this is

A draft built for awareness and tabletop discussion, not a compliance checklist. It runs side by side with OLRA (original), which maps the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications. Same six roles, reframed agentically.

Owners

  • Primary owner: the role that implements the control.
  • Secondary owner: the role that shares or supports it.
  • “Supply-chain topic” marks a control that is deliberately discussed as a supply-chain question, not as a new role.

Tag: how provable is it?

  • Operational architectural, structural or automatable: isolation, sandboxing, least privilege, kill-switches, continuous logging.
  • Aspirational trust-based, or needs human review per action or per agent. Real guidance, but unproven at scale.
  • inferred no published practitioner critique for this category yet; the tag is inferred by resemblance.

Tag lens after Disesdi Cox’s Angles of Attack critique of the OWASP mitigations.

Phase: when does it apply?

Design before build · Deploy when configured and shipped · Runtime while agents run.

Skill set: who must review it?

  • Engineering builds, configures or deploys a technical control.
  • Detection runs a continuous procedure: logging, scanning, auditing.
  • Architecture defines a boundary, identity model or protocol before anything is built.
  • Governance decides a threshold, approval workflow or vendor-acceptance policy.

In a workshop

  • Tabletop mode: cards show blanks for owner, reliance and verification. Allocate, then reveal.
  • Matrix: where ownership piles up, and which roles barely appear. Click a cell to list its controls.
  • Filters live in the page address: copy the URL to share a view. Cards print two per page width.
